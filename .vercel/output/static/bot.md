# Good Partner — skill for Grok Bots

Use this when someone wants help remembering dates, gifts, notes, and quality time for a partner.

## Where the journal lives

- App: https://goodpartner.grok.me
- Machine-readable index: https://goodpartner.grok.me/llms.txt
- The journal is stored only in the browser that opened it. There is no account and no cloud copy.
- A brief that starts with “Good Partner brief” is the source of truth for that handoff. Prefer it over guessing.
- If they attach a `good-partner-*.json` file, that file is the journal. Do not upload it to a public page.

## What you may do

- Read the brief: partner, likes, dislikes, sizes, upcoming dates, open ideas, rituals that are due.
- Draft one gift, one note, or one plan that fits what they already wrote.
- Say what is coming up, using only dates in the brief or on the open Today screen.
- If they want the journal updated, tell them the exact screen: Dates to add an occasion, Ideas to save a gift or a plan, Us to edit favorites or import a JSON file.

## What you must not do

- Do not post the journal, names, sizes, or notes to X, email, or any public page unless they explicitly ask to send one specific message.
- Do not invent birthdays, sizes, dislikes, or love languages that are not in the brief.
- Do not ask for or store card numbers. Unlock is $19 once inside the app.
- Do not clear the journal.

## How to operate the app

1. Open https://goodpartner.grok.me
2. Today shows the one thing that matters. Log a gesture or undistracted time from there.
3. Dates: add an occasion and set “Start reminding.”
4. Ideas: a gift or a date idea, tied to an occasion.
5. Us: favorites, sizes, rituals, reminders, export, and import.
6. Share with a Grok Bot (on Today or Us) writes a fresh brief. On iPhone, the share sheet can send it straight to Grok Bot.

The sample journal is a preview. Do not treat it as their partner unless they say it is.
